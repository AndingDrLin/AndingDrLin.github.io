## Question 1 (10 points)

Based on the principle of conservation of charge and the definition of divergence, prove the divergence theorem $\iiint_V (\nabla \cdot \vec{F}) dV = \iint_S \vec{F} \cdot d\vec{S}$. Then, by the divergence theorem, calculate the electric field intensity $\vec{E}$ produced by a cylindrical charge distribution of radius $R$ and uniform density $\rho$.

## Question 2 (10 points)

(1) State the relationship between electric field intensity $\vec{E}$ and potential $V$, then use this relation to calculate $\vec{E}$ produced by a point charge $q$. State the electrostatic boundary conditions. Let the x-y plane be the boundary between two dielectrics with $\varepsilon_1 = 3\varepsilon_0$ for $z < 0$ and $\varepsilon_2 = 6\varepsilon_0$ for $z > 0$. If $\vec{E}_1 = \vec{a}_x 12 + \vec{a}_y 9 + \vec{a}_z 2$ for $z < 0$, find $\vec{E}_2$.

(2) State Gauss's law and derive the electric field $\vec{E}$ using Gauss's law for an infinitely long line charge with uniform charge density $\rho_L$. A coaxial line with inner radius $a$ and outer radius $b$ has an applied voltage $U$. What is the electric field intensity between the conductors? What is the formula for the capacitance of the coaxial line?

## Question 3 (10 points)

(1) State the Lorentz force equation and discuss the motion of a charged particle in a uniform static magnetic field.

(2) A rectangular loop of wire with sides $a$ and $b$ carries a current $I$. The loop is placed in a uniform magnetic field $\vec{B}$. What is the torque on the loop? Write the detailed solution.

## Question 4 (10 points)

(1) What is the magnetic vector potential? Explain its relation with the current distribution.

(2) What is the magnetic flux density $\vec{B}$ produced by a surface current sheet $J_S = \vec{a}_y J_S$ at $z = 0$? Write the detailed solution.

## Question 5 (10 points)

What is the polarization of a uniform plane wave? Determine the polarization and direction of propagation for each of the following cases:

(a) $\vec{E} = (\vec{a}_x 3 + \vec{a}_z 2)e^{-j\beta y}$

(b) $\vec{E}(z,t) = \vec{a}_x \cos(\omega t - \beta z) - \vec{a}_y \sin(\omega t - \beta z)$

(c) $\vec{E}(z,t) = \vec{a}_x \cos(\omega t - \beta z + \pi/4) - \vec{a}_y \cos(\omega t - \beta z)$

## Question 6 (10 points)

(1) A plane wave propagates in a general lossy medium. What is the general expression of the electric field? What is the difference between a good conductor and a good dielectric? How to define the skin depth? Write the solutions.

(2) The conductivity of sea water is $\sigma = 4$ S/m, $\varepsilon_r = 81$, $\mu_r = 1$. A plane wave with frequency $f$ propagates in sea water. What is the frequency $f$ for the distance to be 1 m? Write the detailed solution.

## Question 7 (10 points)

(1) State the reflection and transmission coefficients for a uniform plane wave at normal incidence on a plane conducting boundary. What is the standing wave ratio?

(2) A uniform plane wave in air with $\vec{E}_i = \vec{a}_x 10e^{-j6z}$ is normally incident on a lossless medium ($\sigma = 0$, $\mu_r = 1$, $\varepsilon_r = 2.5$) surface at $z = 0$. Find: (a) $\lambda$, $f$; (b) the reflected and transmitted magnetic fields.

## Question 8 (10 points)

An air-filled rectangular waveguide has dimensions $a = 6$ cm and $b = 4$ cm. A signal with frequency $f = 6$ GHz propagates inside the guide. Determine:

(a) Which modes can propagate in the waveguide?

(b) Calculate the phase constant $\beta$, the guide wavelength $\lambda_g$, the phase velocity $v_p$, and the group velocity $v_g$ for the dominant mode.

(c) Calculate the wave impedance for the dominant mode.

(d) Determine the maximum power that can be transmitted (assuming the maximum electric field intensity in the guide is $3 \times 10^6$ V/m).

## Question 9 (10 points)

(a) The magnetic field in a source-free region is given by $\vec{H} = \vec{a}_x H_0 \sin(\pi y) e^{-jkz}$. Determine the displacement current density and the associated electric field.

(b) Two parallel conducting plates are located at $x = 0$ and $x = d$. The potential $V(x) = V_0 x/d$. The region $0 < x < d$ is filled with a dielectric medium of permittivity $\varepsilon$. The plates have an area of $S$. Find the boundary conditions and the capacitance between the plates.

(c) What is the induced EMF in a closed loop? State Faraday's law and calculate the induced EMF in a rectangular loop of dimensions $a \times b$ located in a time-varying magnetic field $\vec{B} = \vec{a}_z B_0 \cos(\omega t)$.

(d) Find the inductance per unit length of a coaxial line with inner radius $a$ and outer radius $b$ filled with a medium of permeability $\mu$.

## Question 10 (10 points)

A uniform plane wave with frequency $f = 100$ MHz propagates in a medium with $\varepsilon_r = 9$, $\mu_r = 1$. The electric field amplitude is 10 V/m.

(a) Find the phase constant, wavelength, phase velocity, and wave impedance.

(b) Determine the direction of propagation, the polarization of the electric field, and the time-domain expression of the magnetic field.

(c) When this wave is incident normally on the surface of a perfect conductor at $z = 0$, find the reflected wave electric field, the total electric field in the region $z < 0$, and the standing wave ratio.

(d) A rectangular waveguide is filled with this medium. If $a > b$, which is the dominant mode? What is the cutoff frequency of the dominant mode? What are the conditions for this waveguide to be a single-mode waveguide?
