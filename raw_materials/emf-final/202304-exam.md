## Question 1 (10 points)

(1) Explain the physical meaning of the electric flux density $\vec{D}$. Can we say that $\vec{D}$ is determined only by the distribution of free charge? Please use examples to support your answer.

(2) Use Gauss's law to determine the electric field produced by an infinite line charge with uniform charge density $\rho_L$.

(3) Find the total charge within the surface $r = 0.05$ m, given that $\vec{D} = \frac{0.1}{r} \vec{a}_r$ C/m².

(4) Let $V = 2xy^2z^3 + 3 \ln(x^2 + 2y^2 + 3z^2)$, determine $\nabla^2 V$.

## Question 2 (10 points)

(1) What is the electrostatic boundary condition for the normal components of $\vec{E}$ at the boundary of two different dielectric media?

(2) A coaxial line is filled with a dielectric medium ($\mu$, $\varepsilon_1$, $\sigma_1$) between $a < r < c$, and another dielectric medium ($\mu$, $\varepsilon_2$, $\sigma_2$) between $c < r < b$. Find the resistance per unit length of the two regions.

(3) State Stokes' theorem.

(4) The magnetic field between the conductors of a coaxial line is $\vec{H} = \frac{I}{2\pi \rho} \vec{a}_\phi$. Find the curl of $\vec{H}$.

## Question 3 (10 points)

(1) What is transformer EMF? In a solenoid of length $L$ and radius $a$, the number of turns per unit length is $n$. Find the mutual inductance.

(2) State the boundary conditions for the normal components of the magnetic flux density $\vec{B}$ at the boundary of two different magnetic media.

(3) A long solenoid has $N$ turns and carries a current $I$. Find the inductance.

## Question 4 (10 points)

(1) Let $V = \frac{\rho_0}{4\varepsilon_0}(a^2 - \rho^2)$ for $\rho < a$ and $V = 0$ for $\rho > a$. Find the charge distribution $\rho_v$.

(2) Find the electric field $\vec{E}$ for all regions.

(3) The boundary is at $z = 0$. Medium 1 ($z < 0$) has $\varepsilon_1$, and Medium 2 ($z > 0$) has $\varepsilon_2$. There is a surface charge $\rho_s$ on the boundary. Find the boundary conditions.

(4) Find the capacitance per unit length of a coaxial line with inner radius $a$ and outer radius $b$.

(5) A charge $q$ is located at $(0, 0, h)$ above a grounded conducting plane at $z = 0$. Find the image charge and the electric field at a point on the $z$-axis.

## Question 5 (10 points)

(1) What is a good conductor? For a plane wave propagating in a good conductor, derive the expressions for the attenuation constant $\alpha$ and the skin depth $\delta$.

(2) Find the skin depth of copper at $f = 10^8$ Hz ($\sigma = 5.8 \times 10^7$ S/m, $\mu_r = 1$, $\varepsilon_r = 1$).

(3) What are the differences between electromagnetic wave propagation in a good conductor and a lossless dielectric?

## Question 6 (10 points)

(1) The electric field of a uniform plane wave is $\vec{E} = \vec{a}_x E_0 e^{-jky} + \vec{a}_z E_0 e^{-jky}$. What is the polarization?

(2) $\vec{E}(z,t) = \vec{a}_x E_0 \sin(\omega t + kz) + \vec{a}_y E_0 \cos(\omega t + kz)$. Find the polarization and the direction of propagation.

(3) What is the polarization of $\vec{E}(z) = (\vec{a}_x 3 - \vec{a}_z 2)e^{-j\beta y}$?

(4) Describe the different types of polarization.

## Question 7 (10 points)

(1) A uniform plane wave is incident normally from air ($z < 0$) onto a lossless dielectric medium ($\varepsilon_r = 4$, $z > 0$). If $\vec{E}_i = \vec{a}_x 10 e^{-j4z}$, find: (a) $\lambda$; (b) $\Gamma$ and $\tau$; (c) the reflected and transmitted electric fields; (d) the reflected and transmitted magnetic fields.

(2) What is the standing wave ratio?

## Question 8 (10 points)

(1) What is the Smith chart? Explain the two sets of circles on the Smith chart and state its main uses.

(2) The normalized load impedance is $Z_L = 1 - j$. Find the normalized load admittance $Y_L$ and the reflection coefficient $\Gamma$ using the Smith chart.

(3) A lossless transmission line has characteristic impedance $Z_0 = 50\ \Omega$ and load $Z_L = 100 + j50\ \Omega$. Use the Smith chart to find the VSWR and the distance from the first voltage minimum to the load.

## Question 9 (10 points)

(1) A rectangular waveguide has dimensions $a = 7$ cm, $b = 3$ cm. Determine the cutoff frequency of the dominant mode for the following cases: (a) air-filled; (b) filled with dielectric $\varepsilon_r = 4$, $\mu_r = 1$.

(2) An air-filled rectangular waveguide has dimensions $a = 6$ cm, $b = 4$ cm. The operating frequency is $f = 6$ GHz. For the dominant mode: (a) find the cutoff frequency; (b) find $\beta$, $\lambda_g$, $v_p$, and $v_g$; (c) find the wave impedance.

## Question 10 (10 points)

(1) For a two-port microwave network, explain how to determine the [Z], [Y], and [S] matrices.

(2) What is a two-hole directional coupler? Explain its principle of operation.

(3) What is a magic tee? How does it couple power? What is the difference between a magic tee and a two-hole directional coupler?
