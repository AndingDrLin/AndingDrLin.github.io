## Question 1 (10 points)

(1) State Gauss's law in electrostatics and derive the electric field intensity $\vec{E}$ produced by an infinitely long uniform line charge $\rho_L$ using Gauss's law.

(2) If the electric field intensity is $\vec{E} = \vec{a}_r 10r^2 + \vec{a}_z 3z$ (in free space, unit: V/m), find the electric flux density $\vec{D}$ and the volume charge density $\rho_v$ at $r = 0.1$ m.

(3) The boundary between two dielectric media is at $z = 0$: Medium 1 ($z < 0$) has permittivity $\varepsilon_1$, Medium 2 ($z > 0$) has permittivity $\varepsilon_2$. There is a surface charge density $\rho_s$ on the boundary. In Medium 1, the electric field is $\vec{E}_1$ and it makes an angle $\theta_1$ with the boundary normal. Find $\vec{E}_2$ in Medium 2.

(4) If $\vec{E} = \vec{a}_r 5r \cos\phi$, find the volume charge density $\rho_v$.

## Question 2 (10 points)

(1) State the definition of resistance and derive the formula $R = \rho l / S$.

(2) A coaxial capacitor consists of two concentric cylindrical conductors with inner radius $a$ and outer radius $b$. The region $a < r < c$ is filled with a medium of conductivity $\sigma_1$ and permittivity $\varepsilon_1$, and the region $c < r < b$ is filled with a medium of conductivity $\sigma_2$ and permittivity $\varepsilon_2$. Find the capacitance of this capacitor.

(3) A sphere of radius $a$ is filled with uniform volume charge density $\rho$. Use Gauss's law to find $\vec{E}$ for $r < a$ and $r > a$.

(4) A coaxial line has inner radius $a$, outer radius $b$, and is filled with a medium of permittivity $\varepsilon$. The voltage between the conductors is $U$. Find the capacitance per unit length.

## Question 3 (10 points)

(1) What is the relationship between $\vec{E}$ and $\vec{D}$? State Gauss's law and use it to find the electric field $\vec{E}$ between the conductors of a coaxial line with inner radius $a$, outer radius $b$, and uniform line charge density $\rho_l$.

(2) Let $V = 2xy^2z^3 + 3\ln(x^2 + 2y^2 + 3z^2)$. Find $\nabla^2 V$.

(3) What are the electrostatic boundary conditions at the interface between two dielectric media?

## Question 4 (10 points)

(1) State Faraday's law of electromagnetic induction and explain the concept of transformer EMF.

(2) A solenoid has $N$ turns, length $l$, and radius $a$. Find the mutual inductance.

(3) What are the boundary conditions for the normal component of the magnetic flux density $\vec{B}$ at the interface of two different magnetic media?

(4) A long solenoid has $N$ turns and length $l$. Find the inductance.

## Question 5 (10 points)

(1) What are the current $I$ and the current density $\vec{J}$? What are their units? What is the relationship $\nabla \cdot \vec{J} = -\frac{\partial \rho_v}{\partial t}$? Explain the physical meaning.

(2) A parallel plate capacitor has two dielectrics: Medium 1 ($\varepsilon_1$, $\sigma_1$) with thickness $d_1$ and Medium 2 ($\varepsilon_2$, $\sigma_2$) with thickness $d_2$, plate area $S$. Find the resistance $R$.

(3) What is the resistance per unit length of a coaxial line with inner radius $a$, outer radius $b$, and medium conductivity $\sigma$?

## Question 6 (10 points)

(1) State Faraday's law and explain the two types of EMF: transformer EMF and motional EMF.

(2) A uniform plane wave with frequency $f = 1$ MHz propagates in a good conductor with $\sigma = 5.8 \times 10^7$ S/m. Find the skin depth $\delta$ and the attenuation constant $\alpha$.

(3) What is the wave impedance $\eta_c$ of a good conductor?

## Question 7 (10 points)

(1) What is the polarization of a uniform plane wave?

(2) Determine the polarization of $\vec{E} = (2\vec{a}_x + \vec{a}_z)e^{-j\beta y}$.

(3) A uniform plane wave is normally incident on the surface of a perfect conductor. Describe the reflected wave and the standing wave.

## Question 8 (10 points)

(1) Explain the principle of the Smith chart. What are the normalized impedance and normalized admittance?

(2) A lossless transmission line with characteristic impedance $Z_0 = 100\ \Omega$ is terminated by $Z_L = 50 + j50\ \Omega$. Use the Smith chart to find the VSWR and the position of the first voltage minimum from the load.

(3) A lossless transmission line with $Z_0 = 50\ \Omega$ is terminated by $Z_L = 60 - j80\ \Omega$. Design a single-stub matching network: find the position of the stub from the load and the length of the short-circuited stub.

## Question 9 (10 points)

(1) An air-filled rectangular waveguide has dimensions $a = 7.2$ cm and $b = 3.4$ cm. Find the cutoff frequencies of the TE$_{10}$, TE$_{20}$, and TE$_{01}$ modes.

(2) For the TE$_{10}$ mode with operating frequency $f = 3$ GHz, find $\beta$, $\lambda_g$, $v_p$, $v_g$, and the wave impedance $Z_{TE}$.

(3) What is the maximum time-average power capacity of the TE$_{10}$ mode?

## Question 10 (10 points)

(1) Derive the input impedance $Z_{in}$ of a lossless transmission line with characteristic impedance $Z_0$, length $l$, and load impedance $Z_L$.

(2) A $\lambda/4$ impedance transformer with characteristic impedance $Z_0 = 50\ \Omega$ is connected to a load $Z_L = 125\ \Omega$. Find the input impedance.

(3) A lossless transmission line with $Z_0 = 50\ \Omega$ has VSWR $= 2.5$ and the first voltage minimum at $0.15\lambda$ from the load. Find the load impedance $Z_L$ and the reflection coefficient $\Gamma$.
