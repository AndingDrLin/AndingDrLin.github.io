## Question 1 (10 points)

(1) State Gauss's law. Use Gauss's law to determine the electric field $\vec{E}$ produced by an infinite line charge with uniform charge density $\rho_L$.

(2) What is an image charge? What is the purpose of using the image charge method? Write the force on a point charge $Q$ located at $(0, 0, h)$ above a grounded infinite conducting plane at $z = 0$. *(8 points)*

## Question 2 (10 points)

(1) Let $\vec{A} = \vec{a}_x (x^2 - 2xy) + \vec{a}_y (2xz - 3yz) + \vec{a}_z (4xy^2 z^2)$. Find the divergence $\nabla \cdot \vec{A}$ and the curl $\nabla \times \vec{A}$.

(2) State Stokes' theorem. *(4 points)*

(3) Two coaxial conductors have radii $a = 2$ mm and $b = 8$ mm. The region $a < \rho < b$ is filled with $\sigma_1 = 10^{-5}$ S/m for $a < \rho < c$ ($c = 4$ mm) and $\sigma_2 = 4 \times 10^{-5}$ S/m for $c < \rho < b$. Find the resistance per unit length. *(6 points)*

## Question 3 (10 points)

(1) What is the relationship between current and current density? What is the unit of current density? *(2 points)*

(2) What are the boundary conditions for current density at the interface of two media with conductivities $\sigma_1$ and $\sigma_2$ and permittivities $\varepsilon_1$ and $\varepsilon_2$? *(4 points)*

(3) Two parallel conducting plates with area $S$ are separated by $d$. The region $0 < z < d_1$ has conductivity $\sigma_1$ and the region $d_1 < z < d$ has conductivity $\sigma_2$. Find the resistance between the plates. *(4 points)*

## Question 4 (10 points)

(1) What is the magnetic vector potential $\vec{A}$? For a surface current $\vec{K}$ on a conducting strip (width $a$, infinite length in $z$), write the expression for $\vec{A}$ produced by the current. *(3 points)*

(2) For a magnetic dipole with magnetic moment $\vec{m}$ in a uniform magnetic field $\vec{B}$: (a) find the force; (b) find the torque. *(3 points)*

(3) Find the magnetic flux density $\vec{B}$ for $\rho < a$ and $\rho > a$ for the surface current distribution $\vec{K} = \vec{a}_\phi K_0$ at $\rho = a$. *(2 points)*

(4) Find the inductance per unit length of a coaxial line with inner radius $a$ and outer radius $b$. *(2 points)*

## Question 5 (10 points)

(1) What is the Lorentz force equation? Explain the motion of a charged particle in uniform static electric and magnetic fields when $\vec{v} \perp \vec{B}$. *(2 points)*

(2) A rectangular loop with dimensions $0.3 \times 0.4$ m carrying current $I = 10$ A is in the field $\vec{H} = \vec{a}_x 3x^2$. Find the magnetic force on each segment of the loop. *(4 points)*

(3) What are the boundary conditions for the tangential and normal components of the magnetic field $\vec{H}$ and the magnetic flux density $\vec{B}$ at the interface of two magnetic media? *(4 points)*

## Question 6 (10 points)

(1) For a uniform plane wave propagating in the $z$ direction in a good conductor, the magnetic field is given. Derive: the attenuation constant $\alpha$, phase constant $\beta$, wavelength $\lambda$, phase velocity $v_p$, skin depth $\delta$, and wave impedance $\eta_c$. *(5 points)*

(2) What is the definition of the Poynting vector? What is the physical meaning of its time average? For a plane wave in a good conductor, derive the average power density. *(5 points)*

## Question 7 (10 points)

(1) A plane wave propagates in air with electric field $\vec{E} = \vec{a}_x 30\pi e^{-j4y}$. Find: (a) the magnetic field $\vec{H}$; (b) the frequency $f$ and wavelength $\lambda$; (c) the average power density. *(6 points)*

(2) Determine the polarization of the following waves:

$$\vec{E} = (\vec{a}_x + \vec{a}_y 3)e^{-j\beta z}$$

$$\vec{E} = (j\vec{a}_x + \vec{a}_y 2)e^{-j\beta z}$$

$$\vec{E} = (\vec{a}_x + j\vec{a}_y)e^{-j\beta z}$$

## Question 8 (10 points)

(1) What is the Smith chart? What are the normalized impedance and normalized admittance? *(3 points)*

(2) A lossless transmission line with $Z_0 = 100\ \Omega$ has VSWR $= 2.0$ and the first voltage maximum at $z = 0$. The line length is $l = 0.5\lambda$. Find the input impedance $Z_{in}$ using the Smith chart. *(3 points)*

(3) A lossless transmission line with $Z_0 = 50\ \Omega$, $l = 2.2\lambda$, is terminated by $Z_L = 50 + j50\ \Omega$. Use the Smith chart to find $Z_{in}$. *(4 points)*

(4) Explain the quarter-wave transformer for impedance matching. What is the condition for matching? What is the characteristic impedance of the quarter-wave section? How does bandwidth affect the matching? *(4 points)*

## Question 9 (10 points)

(1) The magnetic field intensity of a TE$_{10}$ mode in an air-filled rectangular waveguide ($a = 2.286$ cm, $b = 1.016$ cm) is given as:

$$\vec{H} = \vec{a}_y H_0 \cos\left(\frac{\pi}{a} x\right) e^{-j\beta z}$$

Find: (a) the electric field $\vec{E}$; (b) the cutoff frequency $f_c$; (c) the cutoff wavelength $\lambda_c$; (d) the wave impedance $Z_{TE}$. *(4 points)*

(2) A rectangular waveguide is filled with medium $\mu$, $\varepsilon$. Derive the cutoff frequency $f_c$ for the TE$_{mn}$ mode. *(3 points)*

(3) What are the basic parameters of a rectangular waveguide (cutoff frequency, guide wavelength, phase velocity, group velocity, wave impedance)? *(3 points)*

(1) What is the Smith chart? *(3 points)*

(2) A lossless transmission line with $Z_0 = 50\ \Omega$, $l = 0.2\lambda$, is terminated by $Z_L = 100 + j50\ \Omega$. Use the Smith chart to find $Z_{in}$ and VSWR. *(4 points)*

(3) A lossless transmission line with characteristic impedance $Z_0$ is connected to a load $Z_L$. Derive the input impedance formula. *(3 points)*
