Fundamentals
• DC-AC Converters are known as inverters
• Role is to convert a DC signal to AC
• Ideally, output should be sinusoidal.
• In reality, these are non-sinusoidal and contain
  harmonics
• This is fine for low and medium power applications
• Divided into two main types
   • Single Phase
   • Three Phase
• Semiconductor devices typically used
• The basic building block is the ‘Bridge’ circuit
