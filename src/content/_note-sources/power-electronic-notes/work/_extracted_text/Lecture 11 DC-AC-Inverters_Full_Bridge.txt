• The Unipolar scheme has the advantage of “effectively” doubling the switching
  frequency as far as the output harmonics are concerned, compared to the bipolar
  voltage-switching scheme.
• The advantage of “effectively” doubling the switching frequency appears in the
  harmonic spectrum of the output voltage waveform, where the lowest harmonics
  appear as sidebands of twice the switching frequency.
