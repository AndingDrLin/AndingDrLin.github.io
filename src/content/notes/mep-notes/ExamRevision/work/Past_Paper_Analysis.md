# Past Paper and Practice Analysis

## 资料类型

- `Exam paper2022(1).docx`：最高价值 past exam，确定问答与热管理计算题型。
- `Example_PracticeQuestions_MEP2026(1).docx`、`PracticeQuestions_ME2025.docx`：practice with expected answers，用于确定标准答案组织方式。
- `questions_answers.docx`：综合问答，用于补答题措辞。
- `考前一小时.docx`、`绝密级.docx`、`超超整理/`：学长/考前材料，只作为考试信号和遗漏检查。

## 高频题型

| 题型 | 高频主题 | 答题骨架 |
|---|---|---|
| Explain concept | packaging, microsystem, DFR/DFT, CTE, WLP | definition → why important → mechanism → example |
| Discuss / trace development | technology waves, future trends, 3D/heterogeneous integration | generation/trend → features → drawbacks/challenges |
| Compare | SoC vs SiP, CSP vs flip-chip vs WLP, wire bonding vs TAB vs flip-chip | comparison dimensions: integration, cost, performance, reliability, process |
| Explain with figure | package hierarchy, heat path, CTE mismatch, assembly methods | draw simple cross-section → label functions/failure path |
| Thermal calculation | board heat dissipation, convection coefficient, radiation/conduction | write formula → units → substitution → conclusion |
| Materials mechanism | underfill, solder, TIM, substrate, moisture | material role → property → failure prevented/caused |

## 最高优先级问法

1. Trace the development of microelectronics packaging technology across technology waves.
2. Explain why systems packaging is multidisciplinary.
3. Discuss wire bonding / TAB / flip-chip advantages and disadvantages.
4. Explain CTE mismatch and resulting failure modes.
5. Explain thermal management importance and heat transfer modes.
6. Compare SoC and SiP, and explain why SiP is useful.
7. Compare CSP, flip-chip and WLP; explain WLP cost/time advantage and reliability challenge.
8. Explain the impact of Si processing: low-k ILD, die thinning, TSV/interposer, MEMS.

## 使用边界

Practice answers sometimes over-generalize or repeat wording. 正式笔记以 slides 定义为基础，用 practice 只决定考试常问方式和 answer template。
