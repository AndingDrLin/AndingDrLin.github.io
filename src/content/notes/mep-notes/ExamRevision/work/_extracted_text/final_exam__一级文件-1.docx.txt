The chip integrates electronic components (such as transistors, resistors, capacitors, etc.) and circuit functionality. Interconnects: Interconnects are used to connect different electronic components and circuit functionalities within the chip, forming circuit pat hs. Interconnects are typically made of metal (such as aluminum) or multi-layer metal films. 互连 : 互连用于连接芯片内不同的电子元件和电路功能，形成电路路径。互连通常由金属 ( 如铝 ) 或多层金属薄膜制成。 Pins: Pins are the external connection interfaces of the IC, used for connecting with external circuits or devices. Through the pins, the IC can transmit input sig nals, output signals, and power supply signals. 引脚 : 引脚是 IC 的对外连接接口，用于与外部电路或设备连接的。通过引脚， IC 可以传输输入信号、输出信号和电源信号。 Package: The package is the protective and enclosing casing for the IC chip. It is typically made of plastic or ceramic material and has pins or solder balls, allowing the IC to be connected with external cir cuits. 封装 : 封装是集成电路芯片的保护和外壳。它允许集成电路与外部电路连接。通常由塑料或陶瓷材料制成，带有引脚或焊料球， These basic components together form a complete integrated circuit, where the electronic components and circuit functionality on the chip are connected through interconnects and enclosed in a package, which is connected with external circuits throug h pins.
