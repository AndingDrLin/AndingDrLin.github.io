Integrated chips with millions/billions of transistors require testing to verify functions. Design for testability (DFT) addresses this by integrating test features into the circuit design. DFT reduces costs by enabling efficient testing during production, incorporating test capabilities directly into the chip. 集成芯片与数百万/数十亿晶体管需要测试来验证功能。可测试性设计(DFT)通过将测试功能集成到电路设计中来解决这个问题。DFT通过在生产过程中实现有效的测试，将测试功能直接集成到芯片中，从而降低了成本。 Traditional practice tests reliability post-fabrication of ICs and system-level packages. If issues arise, costly rebuild and retest occur. Design for reliability aims to proactively address reliability problems during the upfront design process, pre-fabrication. This approach saves time and costs by understanding and resolving reliability issues in advance. 传统的做法是测试集成电路和系统级封装的后期制造可靠性。如果出现问题，将进行昂贵的重建和重新测试。可靠性设计的目的是在前期设计过程中主动解决可靠性问题。这种方法通过提前了解和解决可靠性问题，节省了时间和成本。 频率相关的问题以及未来： 1.
