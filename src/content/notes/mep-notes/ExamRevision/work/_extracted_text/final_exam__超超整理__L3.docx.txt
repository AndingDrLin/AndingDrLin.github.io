Molded Underfill （模压底部填充胶） : Advanced silicon package manufacturers are literally changing the world – enabling devices that are smaller, more powerful, more robust, more affordable. But all these advancements depend on techniques – such as molded underfill, Cu pillars and stacked dies – that make package inspection much more challenging than in the past. Solder: Solder, also known as solder tin, is a conductive material typically made of an alloy, commonly tin. It is a low-melting-point alloy used in the process of joining metal components during soldering, with a melting point lower than that of the materials being soldered. Solders for interconnects (Sn-Pb, Sn-Ag; gold wire bonds), copper lead frames(Kovar, CuBe, Alloy 42), copper traces in substrates; tungsten, molybdenum traces in co-fired ceramics Layer of Tin Lead solder on the lead frame for making easier the PCB assembly process. Lead free finishing with Tin Bismuth plating or Tin Copper dipping can also be used. Thermal interface materials: Thermal interface materials (TIMs) facilitate heat dissipation from electronic devices to the surrounding environment, ensuring the reliable functioning of electronic hardware, communication equipment, and portable electronics. TIMs establish a thermal pathway between the heat source and heat sink. Common materials include Silicon (Si) Rubber, Si Foam, Si Sponge, and Solid Si sheet. substrates : Usually silver-loaded polymers are used, but the term generally encompasses the use of other adhesives, such as polyimide- or silicone-based materials.
