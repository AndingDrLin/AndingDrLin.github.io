L7 What is the difference between CSP, Flip Chip and Wafer-level Packaging? Why do you expect the cost of wafer-level packaging to be lower than with wirebond ? CSP, 倒装芯片和 晶圆 级封装之间的区别是什么 ? 为什么 晶圆级封装 的成本会低于 键线 级封装？ 1. CSP: Package size comparable to chip size, no external leads/pins. Small size, high I/O density. 2. Flip Chip: Direct bonding of chip's active side to substrate/package using solder bumps or conductive bumps. High I/O count, better electrical performance and thermal management. 3. WLP: Packaging processes done at wafer level, integrating packaging within wafer fabrication. Smaller form factor, high I/O density. WLP costs are expected to be lower than wire bonding due to: - Elimination of individual bonding, reducing labor and equipment costs. - Streamlined process integration, minimizing material handling and additional packaging steps. - Yield improvement through early detection of defects at wafer level, reducing scrap and rework costs. - Smaller form factor enabling higher chip production per wafer, increasing throughput and lowering cost per chip. WLP 成本预计低于线粘接，原因如下 :- 消除单独粘合，降低人工和设备成本。 - 简化流程集成，最大限度地减少物料处理和额外的包装步骤。 - 通过早期发现 晶圆级缺陷 来提高良率，减少废品和返工成本。 - 更小的外形尺寸可以提高每片晶圆的芯片产量，提高吞吐量并降低每片芯片的成本。 Flip chip production that has been in place for 30 years has always been on wafer. How is wafer-level packaging being developed differently today? 倒装芯片的生产已经有 30 年的历史，一直都是在晶圆上进行的。 今天晶圆级 封装的发展有何不同？ While flip chip production is traditionally performed at the wafer level, wafer-level packaging (WLP) integrates packaging processes into wafer fabrication. This allows package structures to be formed on the wafer surface before singulation. In contrast to flip chip, WLP performs packaging processes for multiple chips simultaneously on a single wafer. SOC 与 SIP SoC (System on Chip) integrates an entire electronic system onto a single chip, combining functions like microprocessors, memory, optics, oscillators, and RF capabilities. It offers better performance than SiP (System in Package) due to compact connections. However, SoC has high design and prototyping costs, and long development cycles. SiP provides an alternative by incorporating multiple chips into one package, each handling specific functions. SiP is an expansion of the multi-chip-module (MCM) concept, assembling chips on a substrate using wire bonding and flip chip processes. SiP enables vertical and horizontal assembly through 3D die and package stacking, reducing development time and risk. SoC suits high-volume, low-complexity production, while SiP is ideal for low-volume, complex systems. Single-Chip Wafer-Level packaging (SCP, WLP) ： The single-chip WLP is similar to a Chip-scale package (CSP) in package configuration. The main difference between a single-chip WLP and a CSP is the packaging assembly process. Single-chip WLPs are made using wafer-level packaging technology in which the interconnection bumping and testing is performed on the wafer. A single chip package (SCP) is a package that supports a single microelectronic device so that its electrical, mechanical, thermal, and chemical performance needs are adequately served. Wafer-level packaging (WLP) 优势： 1. Smallest system size due to chip-size packaging. 2. Interconnect continuum from IC to PWB facilitated by thin-film processing. 3. Reduced packaging cost as all connections are made at the wafer level. 4. Reduced testing cost as testing is performed at the wafer level for all ICs. 5. Reduced burn-in cost as burn-in is conducted at the wafer level. 6. Elimination of underfill due to compliant leads or other reliability measures. 7. Improved electrical performance due to shorter lead lengths. 8. Size benefits for portable products and cost benefits for all products are the primary drivers of WLP. 缺点： 1. High I/O ICs require small solder balls on tight pitch within the active area of the die. 2. Feasibility of manufacturing small solder balls exists, but it necessitates high-density PWB interconnects, which are expensive. 3. Current board pitch is 0.5 mm (500 microns), but to accommodate 100 micron pitch, significant improvements are required. 4. Achieving 100 micron pitch would necessitate using 25 micron lithography technology on a large board. Wafer-level packaging Reliability （晶圆的可靠性） WLP reliability can be achieved through various methods. The underlying principle of reliability is based on the Coffin-Manson or modified equation, which predicts that the thermal fatigue lifetime of a solder joint is proportional to the square of the bump height or standoff. To enhance reliability, underfills are utilized, which involve filling the gaps with epoxy resins. WLP （重要） WLP is a chip scale package technology, providing the smallest footprint relative to the die size, making it ideal for portable electronics. WLP integrates package processes in the wafer fab, ensuring a streamlined process from silicon start to customer shipment. WLP does not use an interposer, and solder balls are formed on the chip through wafer level processing. The solder ball pad is routed within the chip outline in a "fan-in" design, allowing for a small ball pitch of 0.3 mm. To ensure high yield and reliability when directly mounted on the PCB, a ball pitch larger than 0.4 mm is necessary.
