# Formula Registry（期末复习核心公式登记）

> 目标：为最终复习笔记提供可追踪、网页端 KaTeX 兼容的核心公式。中文为主，关键英文术语保留。  
> KaTeX 使用建议：行内公式用 `$...$`，独立公式用 `$$...$$`。下表中的 `KaTeX 写法` 可直接复制到 Markdown。

| Formula ID | Topic | KaTeX 写法 | 变量定义 | 适用条件 | 常见陷阱 | 来源 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| F-001 | Inductor voltage-current | `$v_L(t)=L\dfrac{di_L(t)}{dt}$` | `$v_L$`：电感电压；`$i_L$`：电感电流；`$L$`：电感量 | 线性电感，采用 passive sign convention | 电感电流不能突变；符号取决于参考方向 | `slides/Lecture 2 Revision of Electric Circuit.pdf`; `slides/Lecture 7 DC-DC Converters_BckGnd_BuckCov.pdf` |
| F-002 | Inductor ripple / volt-second balance | `$\Delta i_L=\dfrac{v_L\Delta t}{L}$`; `$\displaystyle \int_0^T v_L(t)\,dt=0$` | `$\Delta i_L$`：电感电流变化；`$T$`：周期 | 稳态 switching converter，CCM 常用；分段电压近似恒定 | 稳态平均电感电压为零，不代表瞬时电感电压为零 | `slides/Lecture 7 DC-DC Converters_BckGnd_BuckCov.pdf`; `slides/Lecture 8 DC-DC Converters_Boost_BuckBoostConv.pdf` |
| F-003 | Capacitor current-voltage | `$i_C(t)=C\dfrac{dv_C(t)}{dt}$` | `$i_C$`：电容电流；`$v_C$`：电容电压；`$C$`：电容量 | 线性电容，passive sign convention | 电容电压不能突变；电容平均电流在稳态为零 | `slides/Lecture 2 Revision of Electric Circuit.pdf`; `slides/Lecture 7 DC-DC Converters_BckGnd_BuckCov.pdf` |
| F-004 | Capacitor ripple / charge balance | `$\Delta v_C=\dfrac{1}{C}\int i_C(t)\,dt=\dfrac{\Delta Q}{C}$`; `$\displaystyle \int_0^T i_C(t)\,dt=0$` | `$\Delta Q$`：电容电荷变化 | 稳态输出滤波电容，ripple 小时常用近似 | 不要把 capacitor ripple 与 inductor ripple 的三角波面积混淆 | `slides/Lecture 7 DC-DC Converters_BckGnd_BuckCov.pdf`; `materials/Tutorial_2.pdf` |
| F-005 | Average value | `$X_{\mathrm{avg}}=\dfrac{1}{T}\int_0^T x(t)\,dt$` | `$x(t)$`：周期波形；`$T$`：周期 | 任意周期波形 | average 可为正、负或零；整流后平均值与原交流平均值不同 | `slides/Lecture 2 Revision of Electric Circuit.pdf`; `materials/homework.pdf` |
| F-006 | RMS value | `$X_{\mathrm{rms}}=\sqrt{\dfrac{1}{T}\int_0^T x^2(t)\,dt}$` | `$X_{\mathrm{rms}}$`：root mean square | 任意周期波形，尤其用于功率/热效应 | RMS 不是 average；先平方再平均再开方 | `slides/Lecture 2 Revision of Electric Circuit.pdf`; `materials/homework.pdf` |
| F-007 | Sinusoid RMS and offset RMS | `$V_{\mathrm{rms}}=\dfrac{\hat V}{\sqrt{2}}$`; `$V_{\mathrm{rms}}=\sqrt{\dfrac{A^2}{2}+B^2}$` | `$\hat V$`：峰值；`$A\sin(\omega t)+B$` 中 `$A$` 为正弦幅值、`$B$` 为 DC offset | 完整周期正弦波 | peak、peak-to-peak、RMS 不可混用；有 DC offset 时不能只用 `$A/\sqrt{2}$` | `slides/Lecture 2 Revision of Electric Circuit.pdf`; `materials/Review of week 5 and week 6 lectures for final exam.pdf` |
| F-008 | Rectangular pulse average/RMS | `$X_{\mathrm{avg}}=DX_m$`; `$X_{\mathrm{rms}}=X_m\sqrt{D}$` | `$D$`：duty cycle；`$X_m$`：脉冲幅值 | 单极性矩形脉冲，off 区为 0 | RMS 是 `$\sqrt{D}$` 倍，不是 `$D$` 倍 | `slides/Lecture 2 Revision of Electric Circuit.pdf`; `materials/Tutorial_1.pdf` |
| F-009 | Duty cycle | `$D=\dfrac{t_{\mathrm{on}}}{T}=\dfrac{t_{\mathrm{on}}}{t_{\mathrm{on}}+t_{\mathrm{off}}}$` | `$t_{\mathrm{on}}$`：开通时间；`$t_{\mathrm{off}}$`：关断时间 | PWM 和 DC-DC converter 基本定义 | 有些课件用 `$\phi$` 表示 duty cycle；不要与 firing angle `$\alpha$` 混淆 | `slides/Lecture 2 Revision of Electric Circuit.pdf`; `slides/Lecture 7 DC-DC Converters_BckGnd_BuckCov.pdf`; `materials/homework.pdf` |
| F-010 | Instantaneous / average power | `$p(t)=v(t)i(t)$`; `$P=\dfrac{1}{T}\int_0^T p(t)\,dt$` | `$p(t)$`：瞬时功率；`$P$`：平均功率 | 通用功率计算 | 对非正弦/开关波形，不要无条件套 `$VI\cos\phi$` | `slides/Lecture 2 Revision of Electric Circuit.pdf`; `materials/homework.pdf` |
| F-011 | Resistive power | `$P=I_{\mathrm{rms}}^2R=\dfrac{V_{\mathrm{rms}}^2}{R}$` | `$R$`：电阻；`$I_{\mathrm{rms}}$`、`$V_{\mathrm{rms}}$`：RMS 值 | 纯电阻负载或导通电阻损耗 | 必须用 RMS 电流；不要用 average 电流计算热损耗 | `slides/Lecture 2 Revision of Electric Circuit.pdf`; `slides/Lecture 6 Heatsinks.pdf` |
| F-012 | Diode conduction loss | `$P_D\approx V_F I_{F,\mathrm{avg}}$` | `$V_F$`：正向压降；`$I_{F,\mathrm{avg}}$`：二极管平均正向电流 | 二极管压降近似恒定 | 若题目给动态电阻模型，应加 `$I_{\mathrm{rms}}^2r_d$` 项 | `slides/Lecture 4 Uncontrolled Switch -Diodes.pdf`; `materials/homework.pdf` |
| F-013 | Diode reverse recovery charge | `$Q_{rr}\approx \dfrac{1}{2}I_{RM}t_{rr}$` | `$Q_{rr}$`：reverse recovery charge；`$I_{RM}$`：最大反向恢复电流；`$t_{rr}$`：恢复时间 | 三角形反向恢复电流近似 | 数据手册给 `$Q_{rr}$` 时通常直接使用，不必重新估算 | `slides/Lecture 4 Uncontrolled Switch -Diodes.pdf`; `materials/Review of week 5 and week 6 lectures for final exam.pdf` |
| F-014 | Diode reverse recovery power loss | `$P_{RR}=Q_{RR}V_R f_s$` | `$V_R$`：blocking/reverse voltage；`$f_s$`：switching frequency | 每周期一次 reverse recovery，硬开关近似 | `$Q_{RR}$` 单位常为 `µC`，频率常为 `kHz`，注意单位换算 | `materials/homework.pdf`; `materials/final_2017.pdf`; `materials/final_2018.pdf` |
| F-015 | MOSFET conduction loss | `$P_{\mathrm{cond}}=I_{D,\mathrm{rms}}^2R_{DS(on)}$` | `$I_{D,\mathrm{rms}}$`：MOSFET 导通 RMS 电流；`$R_{DS(on)}$`：导通电阻 | MOSFET fully-on，电阻模型 | `$R_{DS(on)}$` 随结温升高；不能用 average 电流代替 RMS 电流 | `slides/Lecture 5 Controllable Switches .pdf`; `slides/Lecture 6 Heatsinks.pdf`; `materials/homework.pdf` |
| F-016 | MOSFET switching loss | `$P_{\mathrm{sw}}\approx \dfrac{1}{2}V_{DS}I_D(t_r+t_f)f_s$` | `$V_{DS}$`：关断电压；`$I_D$`：开关电流；`$t_r,t_f$`：上升/下降时间 | 线性电压/电流重叠近似，硬开关 | turn-on 与 turn-off 条件可能不同；题目若分别给能量 `$E_{on},E_{off}$` 应用 `$P=(E_{on}+E_{off})f_s$` | `slides/Lecture 5 Controllable Switches .pdf`; `slides/Lecture 6 Heatsinks.pdf`; `materials/homework.pdf` |
| F-017 | Total semiconductor loss | `$P_{\mathrm{tot}}=P_{\mathrm{cond}}+P_{\mathrm{sw}}+P_{RR}$` | 各项为导通、开关、反向恢复损耗 | 器件热设计与效率估算 | 不同器件损耗项不同；MOSFET 本体二极管可能也有 reverse recovery | `slides/Lecture 6 Heatsinks.pdf`; `materials/homework.pdf` |
| F-018 | Thermal chain / junction temperature | `$T_j=T_a+P\left(R_{\theta JC}+R_{\theta CS}+R_{\theta SA}\right)$` | `$T_j$`：junction temperature；`$T_a$`：ambient；`$R_{\theta JC}$`：junction-case；`$R_{\theta CS}$`：case-sink；`$R_{\theta SA}$`：sink-ambient | 稳态热阻模型 | 热阻单位是 `°C/W` 或 `K/W`；多个器件共享 heatsink 时功率要合并 | `slides/Lecture 6 Heatsinks.pdf`; `materials/final_2017.pdf`; `materials/final_2018.pdf` |
| F-019 | Required heatsink thermal resistance | `$R_{\theta SA}\le \dfrac{T_{j,\max}-T_a}{P}-R_{\theta JC}-R_{\theta CS}$` | `$T_{j,\max}$`：允许最大结温 | 选择 heatsink 的初步计算 | 留安全裕量；若结果为负，说明散热方案不可行 | `slides/Lecture 6 Heatsinks.pdf`; `materials/homework.pdf` |
| F-020 | Half-wave rectifier average/RMS | `$V_{DC}=\dfrac{\hat V_m}{\pi}=\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{\pi}V_{AC,\mathrm{rms}}\approx0.45V_{AC,\mathrm{rms}}$`; `$V_{\mathrm{rms}}=\dfrac{\hat V_m}{2}$` | `$\hat V_m$`：交流输入峰值 | 理想 half-wave diode rectifier，电阻负载 | RMS 输出不等于 DC average；二极管压降会降低实际输出 | `slides/Lecture 4 Uncontrolled Switch -Diodes.pdf` |
| F-021 | Full-wave / bridge rectifier average/RMS | `$V_{DC}=\dfrac{2\hat V_m}{\pi}\approx0.90V_{AC,\mathrm{rms}}$`; `$V_{\mathrm{rms}}=V_{AC,\mathrm{rms}}$` | `$\hat V_m=\sqrt{2}V_{AC,\mathrm{rms}}$` | 理想 full-wave rectifier，电阻负载 | Bridge rectifier 每次导通有两个二极管压降 | `slides/Lecture 4 Uncontrolled Switch -Diodes.pdf`; `materials/homework.pdf` |
| F-022 | Rectifier capacitor ripple | `$\Delta V\approx \dfrac{I_{load}}{f_{ripple}C}$`; full-wave: `$f_{ripple}=2f_{line}$` | `$I_{load}$`：负载电流；`$C$`：滤波电容 | 大电容、小 ripple、近似恒流放电 | half-wave 用 `$f_{line}$`，full-wave 用 `$2f_{line}$`；忽略二极管压降会偏乐观 | `slides/Lecture 4 Uncontrolled Switch -Diodes.pdf`; `materials/Tutorial_2.pdf` |
| F-023 | Ripple factor | `$r=\dfrac{V_{r,\mathrm{rms}}}{V_{DC}}$` | `$V_{r,\mathrm{rms}}$`：交流纹波 RMS；`$V_{DC}$`：直流平均值 | 整流滤波输出质量评价 | ripple factor 不是 peak-to-peak ripple 除以 DC | `slides/Lecture 4 Uncontrolled Switch -Diodes.pdf`; `materials/homework.pdf` |
| F-024 | SCR half-wave phase control average | `$V_{DC}=\dfrac{\hat V_m}{2\pi}(1+\cos\alpha)$` | `$\alpha$`：firing angle，`$0\le\alpha\le\pi$` | 单相 half-wave SCR，R load，理想器件 | `$\alpha$` 从自然过零点量起；不要与 duty cycle `$D$` 混淆 | `slides/Lecture 5 Controllable Switches .pdf` |
| F-025 | SCR half-wave phase control RMS | `$V_{\mathrm{rms}}=\hat V_m\sqrt{\dfrac{1}{2\pi}\left(\dfrac{\pi-\alpha}{2}+\dfrac{\sin 2\alpha}{4}\right)}$` | 变量同 F-024 | 单相 half-wave SCR，R load | 积分区间是 `$\alpha$` 到 `$\pi$`；RMS 要平方积分 | `slides/Lecture 5 Controllable Switches .pdf` |
| F-026 | Full-wave SCR with R load average | `$V_{DC}=\dfrac{\hat V_m}{\pi}(1+\cos\alpha)$` | `$\alpha$`：firing angle；`$\hat V_m$`：input peak | 每半周从 `$\alpha$` 到 `$\pi$` 导通，输出整流成正脉冲，R load | 不要与 continuous-current fully controlled bridge 混淆 | `slides/Lecture 5 Controllable Switches .pdf`; `materials/homework.pdf` |
| F-026B | Fully controlled bridge continuous-current average | `$V_{DC}=\dfrac{2\hat V_m}{\pi}\cos\alpha$` | 变量同 F-026 | 单相 fully controlled bridge，电感足够大、电流近似连续 | R load discontinuous conduction 不能直接套；`$\alpha>90^\circ$` 时平均值可为负 | `slides/Lecture 5 Controllable Switches .pdf` |
| F-027 | Buck converter voltage gain | `$V_o=DV_{in}$` | `$V_o$`：输出平均电压；`$V_{in}$`：输入电压 | Ideal buck，CCM，稳态 | 实际有开关/二极管压降；DCM 时不再只由 `$D$` 决定 | `slides/Lecture 7 DC-DC Converters_BckGnd_BuckCov.pdf`; `materials/Tutorial_2.pdf` |
| F-028 | Buck inductor ripple | `$\Delta i_L=\dfrac{(V_{in}-V_o)DT}{L}=\dfrac{(V_{in}-V_o)D}{Lf_s}$` | `$f_s=1/T$` | Ideal buck，CCM | on 与 off 段斜率符号相反、幅值相等；使用 peak-to-peak ripple | `slides/Lecture 7 DC-DC Converters_BckGnd_BuckCov.pdf`; `materials/Tutorial_2.pdf` |
| F-029 | Buck output ripple | `$\Delta v_o\approx \dfrac{\Delta i_L}{8f_sC}$` | `$\Delta i_L$`：inductor peak-to-peak ripple | ESR 忽略，三角电容电流 | 若考虑 ESR，另有 `$\Delta v_{ESR}\approx \Delta i_L R_{ESR}$` | `slides/Lecture 7 DC-DC Converters_BckGnd_BuckCov.pdf`; `materials/Tutorial_2.pdf` |
| F-030 | Boost converter voltage gain | `$V_o=\dfrac{V_{in}}{1-D}$` | 变量同上 | Ideal boost，CCM，稳态 | `$D\to1$` 理论增益趋于无穷但实际不可实现；DCM 不同 | `slides/Lecture 8 DC-DC Converters_Boost_BuckBoostConv.pdf`; `materials/Tutorial_2.pdf` |
| F-031 | Boost inductor ripple | `$\Delta i_L=\dfrac{V_{in}D}{Lf_s}$` | `$L$`：boost inductor | Ideal boost，CCM | off 段电感电压为 `$V_{in}-V_o$`，为负 | `slides/Lecture 8 DC-DC Converters_Boost_BuckBoostConv.pdf`; `materials/Tutorial_2.pdf` |
| F-032 | Boost output ripple | `$\Delta v_o\approx \dfrac{I_oD}{Cf_s}$` | `$I_o$`：输出平均电流 | Boost CCM，忽略 ESR | 输出电容在 switch on 时给负载供电；ripple 与 `$D$` 有关 | `slides/Lecture 8 DC-DC Converters_Boost_BuckBoostConv.pdf`; `materials/Tutorial_2.pdf` |
| F-033 | Inverting Buck-Boost voltage gain | `$V_o=-\dfrac{D}{1-D}V_{in}$` | 负号表示输出极性与输入相反 | Ideal inverting buck-boost，CCM | 考题常问 magnitude，应写 `$|V_o|=D V_{in}/(1-D)$` | `slides/Lecture 8 DC-DC Converters_Boost_BuckBoostConv.pdf`; `slides/Lecture 9 Flyback and Snubber Circuits.pdf` |
| F-034 | Buck-Boost inductor ripple | `$\Delta i_L=\dfrac{V_{in}D}{Lf_s}$` | 变量同上 | Ideal inverting buck-boost，CCM | 与 boost on-time 斜率相似，但输出极性不同 | `slides/Lecture 8 DC-DC Converters_Boost_BuckBoostConv.pdf`; `materials/Tutorial_2.pdf` |
| F-035 | Flyback converter voltage gain | `$\dfrac{V_o}{V_{in}}=\dfrac{N_s}{N_p}\dfrac{D}{1-D}$` | `$N_p,N_s$`：primary/secondary turns | Ideal flyback，CCM，按幅值表示 | Flyback transformer 是 coupled inductor；要注意极性 dot convention | `slides/Lecture 9 Flyback and Snubber Circuits.pdf` |
| F-036 | Flyback reflected voltage | `$V_{R}=\dfrac{N_p}{N_s}(V_o+V_D)$` | `$V_R$`：primary reflected voltage；`$V_D$`：二极管压降 | 估算 MOSFET 关断电压应力 | 漏感尖峰需 snubber/clamp，不能只看理想 reflected voltage | `slides/Lecture 9 Flyback and Snubber Circuits.pdf` |

| F-038 | SPWM modulation indices | `$m_a=\dfrac{\hat V_{control}}{\hat V_{tri}}$`; `$m_f=\dfrac{f_s}{f_1}$` | `$m_a$`：amplitude modulation ratio；`$m_f$`：frequency modulation ratio | Sinusoidal PWM，linear region 通常 `$0\le m_a\le1$` | over-modulation 时线性关系失效；`$f_s$` 是 carrier frequency | `slides/Lecture 10 - DC-AC-Inverters_Half_Bridge.pdf`; `slides/Lecture 11 DC-AC-Inverters_Full_Bridge.pdf`; `materials/homework.pdf` |
| F-039 | Half-bridge inverter square-wave RMS | `$V_{o,\mathrm{rms}}=\dfrac{V_d}{2}$` | `$V_d$`：DC link voltage；输出在 `$\pm V_d/2$` 间切换 | Ideal half-bridge square-wave output | 这是输出总 RMS，不是 fundamental RMS | `slides/Lecture 10 - DC-AC-Inverters_Half_Bridge.pdf` |
